About FindIt SDSU

Small moments of help, made easier to find.

We are a three-person CS 514 team building a database-backed lost-and-found concept for the SDSU community.

Student-builtPhase 1Independent project

Our mission

Give every useful detail a place to meet.

Finding a lost item often depends on someone remembering a color, a date, a place, or a tiny distinguishing mark. FindIt SDSU gives those clues a shared structure.

The project began as a CS 514 database assignment. We chose a problem that could be explained through real workflows and strong data rules: a report can have photos and a location; a found item can have claims; only one claim can be approved.

For Phase 1, we use fictional reports in a local demo. The public website presents the project and its evidence; it does not collect lost-item reports or claims.

Our company concept

FindIt Studio is a classroom idea.

For the project presentation, we imagine a small software studio focused on practical campus tools: products that are easy to understand and careful with personal information.

This is a hypothetical company identity for a course assignment. FindIt Studio is not a registered business or an SDSU service. The application remains a local proof of concept rather than a public lost-and-found system.
