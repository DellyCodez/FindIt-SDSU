Why FindIt exists

Good things deserve
a way back.

Lost and found works best when the right details meet in the same place. We built FindIt to make that easier.

Our mission

One item. Too many places to look.

A bottle left at the library might be mentioned in a group chat. Its owner might search somewhere else entirely. A color, a date, and a small mark could connect those stories, if they are easy to compare.

FindIt brings scattered reports into one searchable place, so students can share what they know and compare possible matches.

Explore the reports ↗

Our vision

A clearer way back.

We want reconnecting people with lost belongings to feel simple and accessible. Structured reports make important details easier to find; possible matches help people follow a lead; private claims give the review team a careful next step.
