Our mission

One item. Too many places to look.

A bottle left at the library might be mentioned in a group chat. Its owner might be searching a different feed or asking at another desk. Useful details get scattered, and the two reports never meet.

FindIt gives those details a shared home. A location, a date, a photo, or a distinctive mark can help someone decide which report is worth a closer look.

We’re building an independent student prototype for CS 514. It is not an official SDSU service, and a suggested match is a lead to review, not proof of ownership.
