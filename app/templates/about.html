Why we built it

The item isn’t always far away.
The information is.

A bottle turns up at the library. Its owner is still checking a group chat.

Lost belongings get mentioned in social posts, conversations, and disconnected places. The person searching and the person helping might never see the same report.

FindIt brings those reports together. One place to look, share a photo, and follow the details that might connect the two.

Our mission

Make the way back simpler.

Give people a clear place to report, search, and compare, so a lost item has a better chance of finding its owner.

Looking ahead

Built here. Room to grow.

We’re starting with campus lost and found. In time, we’d like to bring the same approach to other campuses and communities, with the verification and safeguards that wider use needs.
