The project

A missing item should not become a scattered search.

A bottle left in the library, a set of keys at the student union, a phone someone picked up: each story begins with a small amount of information. When reports live in separate conversations, it is hard to connect them.

Our CS 514 team built one searchable place to compare fictional reports, follow public clues, and test a private claim workflow. FindIt SDSU is an independent student project, not an official SDSU service.
