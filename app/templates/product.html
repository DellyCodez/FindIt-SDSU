Under the hood

Simple tools, deliberate data rules.

SQLite persists reports across restarts; uploaded photo files are stored beside the database. Only fictional records belong in this demonstration.

Interface
Server-rendered Flask pages, responsive HTML and CSS, and a little JavaScript.
Storage
Six related SQLite tables: users, categories, locations, items, item_photos, and claims.
Images
Pillow validates JPEG, PNG, and WebP uploads and stores processed WebP files.
Matching
Indexed candidate search compares opposite reports. A score is a lead, not an ownership probability.
Claims
Private explanations stay off public pages. SQLite transactions allow at most one approved claim per found item.
