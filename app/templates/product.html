How FindIt works

From lost to a lead.

Search what is here, share what you know, and follow the details that connect two reports.

Find an item →Report an item ↗
01 / Search

Start with what you remember.

Look up a name, brand, or distinctive detail. Narrow results by lost or found, category, and campus location.

Browse items ↗
02 / Report

Give the details a place to live.

Post an item with a location, date, description, and up to three photos. The more specific the clues, the easier it is to compare reports.

Post a report ↗
03 / Connect

See why two reports might fit.

Possible Matches compares compatible items and explains the shared clues. If a found item looks like yours, submit a private detail for review.

Explore found items ↗
04 / Claim

Share a private detail.

If a found item looks like yours, send a clue only the owner would know. Your contact information stays off the public report.

Browse found items ↗
05 / Review

A careful decision.

A reviewer sees the private claim, checks the details, and can approve or reject it. Approved items are marked returned.

Match scores describe similarities in public reports. They do not establish ownership; a claim is reviewed separately.
